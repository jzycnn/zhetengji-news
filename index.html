From an App You're Already Using: Swipe up from the bottom of the screen, pause in the center, then keep dragging the app toward the left or right edge. You can tap its icon from the Home Screen, swipe down to search for it, or swipe leftwards to find it in the App Library. Tap the second app, and it will appear alongside the first. From the Home Screen or App Library: Touch and hold an app icon, then tap Open in Split View. Tap the second app you want to open alongside it from the Home Screen, or using swipe-down search or the App Library. We've found that in certain cases, two windows of the same app also work. The apps are now paired, and the next time you open either one, the other one will open with it automatically.
