The legislation bans wagering company logos on professional sports jerseys, but existing sponsorship arrangements can continue during a five-year transition period, potentially keeping the branding in place until December 31, 2031. Pocock also called on clubs to end their existing jersey sponsorship arrangements “at the earliest opportunity.” Responding on social media, Pocock said the extended transition was incompatible with the government’s stated ambition of separating gambling from Australian sport.
