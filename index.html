The court noticed the slight branding inconsistency. A unanimous three-judge panel ruled on Friday that Kalshi’s sports event contracts are, in substance, sports bets. Because those bets are not “swaps” within the meaning of the Commodity Exchange Act, they do not fall within the Commodity Futures Trading Commission’s exclusive jurisdiction. Nevada can therefore apply its gaming laws. Calling a wager an event contract, the court effectively concluded, does not make it any less of a wager. The ruling threatens the premise that allowed Kalshi to offer sports markets nationwide without collecting state gambling licences like particularly difficult Pokémon. It also creates a direct split with the Third Circuit, which sided with Kalshi against New Jersey in April.
