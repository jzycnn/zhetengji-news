Today, third-party apps lack a supported way to read the names users give their cellular lines, such as "Work" and "Personal," or read and change the preferred line saved for each contact in iOS. The proposed changes would make that information available to eligible apps. For example, someone could use a third-party contact app to choose their work number for calling a colleague. The request was made through Apple's EU interoperability process, which lets developers ask for access to iPhone and iPad features used by Apple's own apps or hardware. Apple operates the process to help meet its obligations under the EU's Digital Markets Act (DMA). The law requires Apple to give third parties access to the same operating system features available to its own services and devices, so competing products can work as seamlessly with the platform. Although this request falls under the EU process, the resulting changes could be made available more widely.
