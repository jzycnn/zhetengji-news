In iOS 27, the new EQ is specifically tied to your AirPods, so it applies to everything you listen to. Your custom EQ profile also carries over to iPad and Mac when you pair your AirPods with them. What You Need Custom EQ requires Apple's H2 chip, so it supports AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 4, and AirPods Max 2. Earlier models, including the original AirPods Max, aren't compatible with the feature. You'll also need current AirPods beta firmware. If the Equalizer option described below doesn't appear, pair your AirPods with your iPhone, tap your AirPods in Settings, then tap AirPods Beta Updates and toggle on the beta updates option. Afterwards, put your AirPods in the case, connect the case to power, and leave it near your unlocked iPhone to let the latest beta firmware install quietly in the background. Illinois expands behavioral health system to include gambling disorder Another key change is the creation of a formal legal definition for gambling disorder. The legislation also gives the Illinois Department of Human Services a wider role. The department will oversee programmes focused on gambling disorder prevention, treatment and recovery support. The law also weaves gambling disorder into Illinois’ existing behavioral health system. Senate Bill 2749 was sponsored by Sen. Julie Morrison in the Senate and Rep. Bob Morgan in the House before winning bipartisan approval in the General Assembly. With the governor’s signature, Illinois joins a growing list of states formally recognizing gambling disorder within public behavioral health systems.
